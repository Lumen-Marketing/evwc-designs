Same business, same section order, same words and the same photographs. Four completely separate builds with no shared components: each one owns its own material, its own type pairing and its own depth device, so they read as four designs rather than four colourways. Every photo and every clip is their own, pulled from the Instagram account. The reels play muted and loop, and pause when they scroll out of view.

The light one. A drafting sheet, ruled continuously from the top of the page to the bottom.

Almost nothing is in a box. A 24px minor rule and a 144px major rule are painted on the page itself, so the grid runs straight through every section seam instead of restarting. Frosted glass on three things only: the nav, a draggable jump menu, and the stat bar over the hero photograph. Services are one large picture beside two stacked, the seven jobs are an accordion strip where the panel under your pointer takes a third of the width, and recent work is a drag filmstrip running off the right edge of the page.

The cinematic one. Film stock: real grain shuffling on a four step gate, a scanline at the threshold of visibility, and raked light shafts across the flat bands.

Type at 152px, and a white card hung across the boundary. The hero photograph carries a draggable smoked glass card and the headline runs enormous beneath it. A white plate and a picture plate hang off the hero into the section below. The three service groups alternate left and right as contained plates on flat near black, the gallery is a seven plate composition that keeps its own rhythm rather than flattening into a grid, and a glass call pill appears once the hero is behind you.

The industrial one. Milled metal: horizontal linisher marks with a specular sweep across every steel face, and a knurled teal field so the two alloys read differently.

Everything is bolted through. Machine guard glazing, square cut and fixed at four corners: a draggable spec panel over the hero, the promise nodes set into the sheared teal field, the review cores inside their own bolted frames. Tread plate on the sheared seams. Services are three milled cards with recessed job lists, and the seven work plates are bolted onto a twelve column grid that never repeats a row.

The poster one. Screen print: a 45 degree halftone with a second screen pulled two pixels off register, because a hand pulled print never lines up.

Ink keylines and hard offsets, not shadows. Every plate carries a 2px keyline and a solid block of colour offset behind it, which is what a misregistered print does. A sunburst runs behind the hero, the statement is a teal plane with white ink screened over the rays, and the acetate cards are printed transparencies pinned onto the sheet rather than glass floating above it. Seven work prints on a twelve column grid, each pulled at a different size.
