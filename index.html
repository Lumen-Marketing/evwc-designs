A two-plane cinematic hero, a bespoke gallery engine and service rows that text a quote. The most custom engineering of the three.

The work at full size. The hero is two planes with no container: the page's black on the left, the footage bleeding off the top, right and bottom on the right, and the headline laid across both so its long line passes in front of the footage's edge. Media sits in lit enclosures with diffused shadows, the work gallery runs the full viewport with one card at full width and its neighbours clipped to slivers either side, and everything resolves out of a blur as it enters. Services are one ruled run of rows, each with a stadium of the job itself and an arrow that texts for that quote.
