The reels are real, and they really auto-play

Two clips were pulled from @eastvalleywindowcleaningllc and re-encoded for the web: a commercial storefront job and a rooftop solar-panel rinse. They are self-hosted MP4s, so they start on their own, loop, and stay muted. Instagram's own embed widget cannot do that. It only ever shows a cover frame with a play button.

A third post turned out to be a static before and after rather than footage. Both halves of it were cut out as separate photographs and are shown as a matched pair, so nothing on the page pretends to be moving when it is not.

Every fact on the page is verified

Services, service area and the owner's name come from their own door hanger and Instagram captions. The 5.0 rating from 4 Google reviews is the real count, and all four reviews are quoted in full.

No invented statistics, and no offer or discount anywhere, because there isn't one running. The "20% off" that was burned into their before/after post falls outside the two crops used here.

What is still needed

A high-resolution logo file. The only version online is a 150px Instagram avatar, so the Arizona mark used here is a clean vector rebuild of it. Drop the real file in and it swaps straight through.

These are homepages only. No contact form yet, so every call to action is a tap-to-call or tap-to-text.
